## Design DNA

Finovate is a calm premium finance/consulting website. It is not loud or editorial like Aurelia. Its strength is trust: open white space, rounded soft cards, forest green, lime highlights, polished business photography, and restrained typography.

The look is modern financial advisory: confident, minimal, clean, personable, and slightly organic because of circular marks, pill buttons, rounded cards, and soft beige backgrounds.

## Palette

- Forest green: `#1C4B42` - primary text, buttons, dark panels.
- Lime accent: `#B4E717` - emphasis words, icons, service cards, logo mark.
- Deep lime: `#92C200` - secondary accent/hover shade.
- Warm off-white: `#F6F5F2` - page bands and card backgrounds.
- White: `#FFFFFF` - main background and card surfaces.
- Body text: `rgba(0,0,0,.8)` - readable but softer than pure black.
- Muted gray: `#69727D` and `#7E7E7E` - captions, meta labels, supporting copy.

Gradients are subtle:
- `linear-gradient(#F6F5F2 0%, #FFFFFF 100%)`
- `linear-gradient(#F6F5F2 50%, #FFFFFF 100%)`

There are no heavy texture overlays. Texture comes from photography, rounded masks, line icons, and soft layering.

## Typography

- Primary font: `Work Sans`, sans-serif.
- H1: about `58px`, weight `500`, line-height `58px`.
- H2: about `48px`, weight `400`, line-height `57.6px`.
- H3/stat titles: about `30px`, weight `500`, line-height `36px`.
- Body: Work Sans regular, relaxed line-height, dark green or softened black.

Typography behavior:
- Sentence case, not all caps.
- Key words in headings turn lime.
- Headings are large but airy, never compressed.
- Buttons use bold Work Sans, pill shaped.

## Layout Flow

1. Header: logo left, centered navigation, circular search and menu/grid icon on the right.
2. Hero intro: white background, large split headline, CTA group on the right.
3. Hero image: full-width wide business meeting photo under the headline.
4. Overlapping stat cards: soft cards sit over the image edge, mixing metrics and a small image tile.
5. Who we are: two-column intro with tabs/number indicators.
6. Services: dark green rounded container with lime cards and masked image shapes.
7. Values: centered heading, four quiet cards with abstract circular icons.
8. Large photo CTA: full-width business image with a floating beige callout card.
9. Team: clean grid of rounded team cards.
10. Trust/advisor block: large image plus lime certification/consultation card.
11. Testimonials: split text and soft quote card, then partner logo row.
12. Insights: beige band with three rounded cards, center report card in dark green/lime.
13. FAQ: two-column FAQ with plus toggles.
14. Newsletter/resources: dark green block with image half.
15. Footer/contact: rounded off-white footer with final CTA and contact details.

## Components and Details

- Border radius is a major signature: `24px`, `16px`, `30px`, circular `50%`, and pill `100px`.
- Buttons are pill-shaped, dark green with lime circular arrow companion.
- Secondary buttons are white/off-white pills with thin borders.
- Cards often have internal nested panels, but feel soft rather than boxed.
- Icons use simple abstract line/circle motifs, mostly green/lime.
- Service cards use lime backgrounds with oversized abstract cutout masks.
- Images are large, bright, people-focused, and business/finance oriented.
- Overlapping cards create depth, especially around the hero image.
- Spacing is generous: sections breathe with large vertical padding.
- Navigation dropdowns exist, but the top-level nav remains minimal.

## Mobile Behavior

- Header simplifies to logo plus search/grid circles.
- Hero stacks: headline, primary CTA row, secondary button, then image.
- The overlapping assets card becomes a vertical card after the image.
- Rounded cards remain prominent; spacing stays generous.
- Font sizes shrink but keep the same calm Work Sans feel.

## How To Adapt For Closing Gap

Use this direction if we want Closing Gap to feel more polished, consulting-led, and trust-heavy than the current Aurelia-inspired bold agency version.

Recommended adaptation:
- Keep Closing Gap content and proof points.
- Replace financial language with talent, outsourcing, technology, marketing, automation.
- Use forest green + lime as a premium trust palette, or blend with Closing Gap blue only as a secondary accent.
- Use owned/Closing Gap team photos or licensed business photos.
- Use rounded metrics: placements, countries, projects, hours saved.
- Preserve Finovate flow: hero, overlapping metrics, who we are, services, values, image CTA, team/trust, testimonials, insights, FAQ, footer.

Avoid copying:
- Exact demo copy.
- Exact logo or brand marks.
- Exact image assets on a live site unless licensed.
